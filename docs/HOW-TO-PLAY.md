# How to play VaVi Lantern

## Your objective

Carry your torch to the gateway at the end of the level. The bridge collapses behind you: walk to rebuild your flame, then sprint to create enough distance from the collapse. Keep both your flame and your lead healthy.

1. Open the game and select **Choose traveler** if you want someone other than Jonah.
2. Select an unlocked level, then **Begin level**.
3. Hold **Sprint** when the bridge approaches. Release it to recover flame once you have room.
4. Jump over gaps/ropes and slide under beams. Wide gaps show the number of timed jumps required.
5. Cross lantern posts to restore your flame. Reach the final gateway to unlock the next level.

## Controls

| Action | Touchscreen | Keyboard player 1 | Keyboard player 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint | Hold playfield or Sprint button | Shift / Right arrow | D |
| Jump | Tap playfield or Jump button | Space / Up arrow | W |
| Air boost | Tap Jump again while airborne | Repeat Jump | Repeat W |
| Slide | Swipe down or use Slide | Down arrow | S |
| Pause | Pause button | Escape | Shared pause |

Time additional jumps while airborne instead of spending every tap immediately. Jump limits: 3 for levels 1–5, 4 for 6–10, 5 for 11–19, 6 for 20–25. Landing restores the full jump allowance. Jumping changes the animation speed, not the game clock.

## Reading the screen

The top-left box shows your traveler in bold, level, distance, flame percentage, points and distance to the collapsing edge. **SPRINT!** indicates a low lead. The torch also visibly shrinks as its percentage drops. Flame reaching zero, a caught-up bridge, a missed gap or an unavoided beam/rope ends the run.

Each level has three flame posts. They refill the torch but are not restart checkpoints. Death always restarts that level from the beginning. Completed levels stay unlocked on that device. Clearing app/browser storage removes progress; website and Android progress are separate.

## Travelers

Jonah is the default adventurer. Junnu is the tortoise, Prem the cheetah, Chinnu the elephant, Joel the ant, and Pranay the stickman. All are selectable. Prem sprints fastest but uses more flame; Junnu and Joel conserve flame. See [Game design](GAME-DESIGN.md) for exact values.

## Playing together

**Two together** gives two stacked bridges on one device. Use separate touch-control rows or the two keyboard layouts above. Both runners share the selected level; the next level unlocks after the run if either reaches the finish.

**Friends & world board** is optional online play. It requires a separately hosted HTTPS game service, which is not publicly deployed yet. When a working service is available, both players enter the same service URL; one creates an invitation and shares its code, the other joins. The service starts both runners after a four-second countdown. Live races do not pause.

## Sound and comfort

Use **Sound on/off** to enable or mute the generated effects. Browser audio starts only after interaction. The interface supports portrait and landscape; use whichever makes the controls comfortable. Reduced-motion preferences suppress opening camera shake and reduce meteor movement, but gameplay still requires movement and timing.
