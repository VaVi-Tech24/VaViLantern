# iPhone and iPad screenshot gallery

Selected from the owner’s September 24, 2026 captures of VaViLantern in Device Hub simulators. The [Android gallery](../2026-09-12-google-play/README.md) guided the selection: home, travelers, gesture guidance, obstacles and changing scenery.

All 14 PNGs are original files copied byte-for-byte: no cropping, resizing, retouching or mockups. The supplied home captures replace the earlier automated home captures.

## App Store upload guide

| Device | Dimensions | App Store Connect slot | Count |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| iPhone 18 Pro Max | 1320 × 2868 portrait; 2868 × 1320 landscape | iPhone 6.9-inch | 8 |
| iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) | 2064 × 2752 portrait | iPad 13-inch | 6 |

Use **View All Sizes in Media Manager** to select the matching slot. The iPhone images do not fit the 6.5-inch slot. Upload in filename order: home, then two gameplay captures, followed by travelers and the remaining views. [Apple screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications).

The selected images show actual visible states: the barrier screenshots show an approaching obstacle, not a completed slide. Near-duplicate jump frames and unsuccessful falls were omitted. The landscape night-home image was omitted because its menu overlaps the runner and flame label. No two-player or iPad landscape capture was supplied. These are simulator captures, not physical-device certification or App Store approval.
