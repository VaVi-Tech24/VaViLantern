# VaVi Lantern 0.2.1 — Timber and tides

Jonah is the default companion, with a transparent sprite on a white selection card and a torch attached to the animated left hand. Existing level unlocks are preserved. The six companions are Jonah, Chikki (stickman), Junnu (tortoise), Prem (cheetah), Chinnu (elephant), and Joel (ant). The three removed companions are no longer selectable. Prem has a longer body, long galloping legs, rounded ears, tear markings, spots, and a long tail.

Weathered wooden planks have narrow seams, grain and nail details. Two longitudinal timber beams and paired braced posts support the bridge; broken sections fall with their supporting wood. A distant river has moving fish and ripples, with more birds and drifting upper clouds.

All 25 crossings are three times their previous length (1,800–3,600 metres). Odd levels start in daylight; even levels start at night. Every seventh obstacle slot contains a 32-metre double-jump break; ordinary 13-metre gaps are more common. Sparse obstacle spacing and restart-from-level-start behavior remain.

Validation: campaign physics, UI lifecycle and orientation smoke checks, room protocol tests, and 150 deterministic completed runs (all 25 levels with all six companions). These automated runs establish solvability, not human difficulty ratings. Android files are a debug-signed testing APK and an unsigned release bundle; Play signing and device testing are still required.
