# VaVi Lantern 0.2.3

Jonah holds his torch in the forward hand. His free rear arm swings independently around the shoulder while the torch grip stays steady. The deck now uses long horizontal boards in two rows with visible seams and grain. Home-screen option labels are bolder. Chikki, the stickman, appears last in the traveler menu.

Verified in the browser and with the campaign, UI smoke, and service checks. Jump physics and saved progress are unchanged from 0.2.2. The APK is a debug-signed device-testing build; the release bundle is unsigned.
