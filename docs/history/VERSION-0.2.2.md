# VaVi Lantern 0.2.2

- Jonah uses a stable upper-body pose and left-hand torch grip, with animated running legs. The flame still responds to its remaining percentage.
- Rounded, shaded clouds replace the thin ellipses, with parallax movement above and below the bridge.
- Jump launch speed increases from 76 to 100, gravity reduces from 320 to 280, and air travel increases from 48 to 64. A single jump covers approximately 46 metres instead of 23. Up to three jumps are available before landing.
- Ordinary breaks are 20 metres; sparse double-jump breaks are 62 metres; occasional triple-jump breaks are 100 metres. Wide breaks display a quiet two- or three-jump cue.
- Planks are twice as wide, with five-pixel visual seams. Decorative seams do not cause falls; actual missing bridge sections do.

Validation includes campaign and triple-jump tests, UI lifecycle and orientation smoke checks, service tests, and 150 completed deterministic runs covering all characters and levels. The Android APK is for device testing; the release bundle remains unsigned.
