VaVi Lantern Android privacy policy

Publisher: VaViTech24
Effective date: September 12, 2026
Contact: vavitech24@gmail.com

Scope

This policy covers the offline Android edition of VaVi Lantern, package com.vavitech24.lantern, starting with build 32. It offers solo play and two-player play on the same device. It does not include online races, accounts or a world leaderboard.

Information on your device

The game saves level progress, completed levels, local scores, selected character and sound and appearance settings in app-local storage. These records remain on your device; the app does not send them to VaViTech24 or third parties.

No app data collection or sharing

This Android edition has no internet permission, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking, in-app purchases or account registration. It does not collect or share personal data off your device. It does not request access to location, contacts, camera or microphone. Game files and this policy are bundled in the app, so first launch and gameplay work offline.

Storage and deletion

Local records remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Clearing storage removes saved progress and settings. Android app backup is disabled. VaViTech24 does not hold a server copy of your game records and cannot recover them.

Support email

If you separately email vavitech24@gmail.com, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include so we can answer your request. Email is handled through Gmail, independently of offline gameplay. Please do not send sensitive information. Contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence; information may need to be retained where legally required. Google's handling of email is described in its privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Intended audience

The intended audience is ages 13 and older. The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not request age, birth date or personal information from players. Contact us if you have a concern about a minor's information in support correspondence.

Website and platform services

The separately hosted browser game and Google Play's own services are outside this offline app's local storage. Visiting the public website or using Google Play involves those services' network and privacy practices; installing the Android app does not cause it to load the browser game.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the Android edition's data practices change. Privacy questions can be sent to vavitech24@gmail.com.
