VaViLantern privacy policy

Publisher: VaViTech24
Effective date: September 24, 2026
Contact: vavitech24@gmail.com

Scope

This policy covers VaViLantern :Serene & Relaxing for Android (starting with build 32) and for iPhone and iPad (starting with version 1.0.0), published by VaViTech24 with app identifier com.vavitech24.lantern. It offers solo play and two-player play on the same device. It does not include online races, accounts or a world leaderboard.

Information on your device

The game saves level progress, completed levels, local scores, selected character and sound and appearance settings in app-local storage. These records remain on your device; the app does not send them to VaViTech24 or third parties.

No app data collection or sharing

The Android and iOS apps have no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking, in-app purchases or account registration. The Android app has no internet permission; the iOS app loads bundled game files and blocks game network connections. It does not collect or share personal data off your device. It does not request access to location, contacts, camera or microphone. Game files and this policy are bundled in the app, so first launch and gameplay work offline.

Storage and deletion

Android: Local records remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Clearing storage removes saved progress and settings. Android app backup is disabled.

iPhone and iPad: Progress, scores and preferences are saved in local WebKit storage. Deleting the app removes its local records. Apple device backups may include app data according to your settings; restoring a backup may restore those records. VaViTech24 does not operate cloud synchronization or hold a server copy of your game records.

Support email

If you separately email vavitech24@gmail.com, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include so we can answer your request. Email is handled through Gmail, independently of offline gameplay. Please do not send sensitive information. Contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence; information may need to be retained where legally required. Google's handling of email is described in its privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Intended audience

The intended audience is ages 13 and older. The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not request age, birth date or personal information from players. Contact us if you have a concern about a minor's information in support correspondence.

Website and platform services

This policy covers the installed mobile apps. The separately hosted browser game, Google Play, Apple App Store, TestFlight and Apple backup services have their own network and privacy practices. Installing either mobile app does not cause it to load the game from the public website.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if either mobile edition's data practices change. Privacy questions can be sent to vavitech24@gmail.com.
